Introduction

JUMPR ("we," "our," or "us") operates the JUMPR mobile application and related websites (together, the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using JUMPR, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

02

Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide and improve the Service:

Account info
Email, name, password
Profile data
Position, shooting hand, experience level
Video recordings
Jump-shot videos submitted for analysis
Analysis data
Form scores, detected issues, progress history
Usage data
App interactions, drill completions, session frequency
Device info
Device type, OS version, app version
We do not collect sensitive personal information such as health records, biometric identifiers used for authentication, financial account numbers, or precise geolocation.
03

How We Use Your Information

To provide the core service. We process your video recordings to analyze shooting form, generate form scores, detect biomechanical issues, and create personalized training drills.

To personalize your experience. Your profile and analysis history help us tailor drills, track progress, and provide coaching through the AI Coach feature.

To improve the service. Aggregated and anonymized usage data helps us understand how players use JUMPR so we can improve features, accuracy, and performance.

To communicate with you. We may send push notifications about training reminders, streaks, and important announcements. You can disable these any time in your device settings.

To process payments. Subscriptions are handled by the Apple App Store and Google Play. We do not directly collect or store your payment card information.

04

How We Handle Your Videos

Your video recordings are central to how JUMPR works. Here is exactly how we treat them:

Processing. Videos you submit are analyzed by our AI engine to evaluate shooting form, producing a score, detected issues, and an annotated overlay.

Storage. Videos and results are stored securely using Firebase Cloud Storage and Firestore, tied to your account, so you can review your history and track progress.

Access. Only you can access your recordings and results through your account. Our team may review anonymized data solely to improve analysis accuracy.

We will never sell your video recordings, use them for advertising, or share identifiable video content publicly without your explicit consent.
05

AI Coach & Conversations

JUMPR includes an AI Coach powered by a third-party AI provider. When you use it:

Conversation data. Your messages and the AI Coach's responses may be processed by our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate replies, and may be stored to maintain your chat history.

Player context. The AI Coach may reference your form score, detected issues, and progress to give relevant advice. This data is sent to the provider only during active conversations.

No model training on your data. Your conversations are not used to train third-party AI models.

06

Third-Party Services

We rely on the following providers to operate JUMPR:

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, database, and cloud storage for app data and video files. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic — AI language model provider for the AI Coach. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat — subscription and in-app purchase management. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — payment processing for subscriptions, handled entirely by these platforms.

We do not sell your personal information to any third party.

07

Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. Videos, analysis results, and profile data remain available to you until you delete them or close your account.

When you delete your account, we remove your personal data and associated content within 30 days, except where we are required to retain certain records to comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes.

08

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information:

Encryption. Data is encrypted in transit using TLS/SSL and at rest using Firebase's built-in encryption.

Authentication. Access to your account is protected by Firebase Authentication with secure session management.

Access controls. Only authorized team members can access backend systems, limited to what is necessary to operate the Service.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

09

Your Rights & Choices

Access your data. View your profile, analysis history, and training data in the app at any time.

Delete your account. Request deletion of your account and all associated data through the app's Settings, or by emailing ryx1014@gmail.com. We process deletion requests within 30 days.

Manage notifications. Enable or disable push notifications through your device settings or in the app.

Cancel subscription. Cancel any time through the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android).

Data portability. Request a copy of your personal data by contacting us.

10

Children's Privacy

JUMPR is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, contact us at ryx1014@gmail.com.
